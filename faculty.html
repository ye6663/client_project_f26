Faculty Hub

Supporting Engaged Learning

Faculty play an important role in helping students connect academic learning with real-world experiences. This page provides resources for supporting students before, during, and after an engaged learning experience.

How Faculty Can Support Students

  • Clarify expectations: Help students understand project goals, responsibilities, and expected outcomes.
  • Encourage communication: Remind students to communicate regularly with teammates, partners, and project stakeholders.
  • Support reflection: Encourage students to think about what they are learning and how the experience connects to their academic goals.
  • Connect students with resources: Direct students to relevant preparation materials, project resources, and support when needed.

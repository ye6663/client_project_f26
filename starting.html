Starting Your Experience

Meet Your Project Partner

Prepare for your first meeting by reviewing the partner's work. Bring questions and choose someone to take notes.

Questions for the First Meeting

  • What problem does the partner want to address?
  • Who will use the final project?
  • What work has already been done?
  • What information can the partner share?
  • What deadlines should the team know about?

After the meeting, share your notes with the partner. Confirm the main decisions and agree on the next steps.

Make a Project Plan

Write down what your team will do and how you will work together. Review the plan with your partner.

Project Scope

Describe the problem, the intended users, and what your team will deliver.

Explain what your team can complete with the time and resources available.

Team Roles

Choose who will handle each task. Discuss everyone's skills and availability.

Set deadlines and choose a shared place for messages and project files.

Resources for Your Plan

Use the Project Scoping Handout and Terms to discuss the project's goals and limits.

Use the Project Management Worksheet to record the scope, team roles, and timeline.
